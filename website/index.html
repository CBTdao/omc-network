Architecture

Three-Tier Network Stack

L1Settlement Layer

BNB Chain mainnet — OMC token issuance, staking smart contracts, DAO governance and final dispute arbitration.

L2Orchestration Layer

Dedicated L2 Rollup — high-frequency node heartbeats, task-allocation state mapping, state-channel settlements and low-cost micropayments.

GPUCompute Grid

Off-chain containerized grid — Docker/Kubernetes sandboxes with CUDA/ROCm acceleration, NVLink inter-GPU links and P2P data transport.

Workflow

Task Distribution & Matching

Every job is scored and routed by a weighted bipartite matching engine.

1

Task Lockup

Requesters lock OMC fee tokens in the escrow contract before dispatch.

2

Smart Scheduling

Nodes matched by VRAM, TFLOPS, bandwidth, latency & reputation score.

3

Sandbox Execution

Jobs run in isolated containers; outputs verified redundantly & via ZK proofs.

Token Utility & Deflationary Mechanics

💠

Compute Payment

Currency for AI training, inference and 3D rendering jobs.

Node Staking

Collateral proportional to hardware tier before receiving jobs.

🗳️

DAO Governance

Vote on protocol parameters and treasury allocation.

🔥

Burn

30% of every 3% protocol fee is burned outright. Slashing burns a further 20%.

AI Compute Station

Buy compute. The seller's stake is the guarantee.

🧾

You set the ceiling

Quote a reference rate and the contract settles at or below it, refunding the difference. No published price list — the station shows the arithmetic, not an offer.

🔒

Stake is the collateral

A provider can only take the job if its staked OMC meets the hardware tier. The same stake pays you back if it fails to deliver.

⚖️

48-hour dispute window

Verified results release payment automatically. Anything contested goes to the 48-hour window, and slashed collateral is split 50% to you, 30% to the treasury, 20% burned.
