OMC Airdrop Is Open

20,000,000 OMC across 1,000,000 slots of 20 OMC each. Free to join — no purchase, no presale. Claiming opens 1 January 2027.

Published 27 October 2026 · updated 30 October 2026

Omniverse Compute has opened its community airdrop. 20,000,000 OMC — two percent of the fixed 1,000,000,000 supply — is reserved for 1,000,000 slots of 20 OMC each, distributed first come, first served. Joining is free: three social actions, no payment, no token purchase, no deposit. Claiming opens with mainnet on 1 January 2027, and each entry carries a flat 0.01 BNB processing fee at that point.

How to take part

  1. Follow @Omniverse_Depin on X.
  2. Retweet and comment on the pinned post. Both are verified on the airdrop page, not on X.
  3. Join the Telegram group.
  4. Connect a BNB Smart Chain wallet on omc.network/airdrop and register up to 5 base entries. Registering is a signature, not a transaction — it costs no gas. Each invited wallet that registers its first entry adds one entry, up to 5 more.

What is free, and what the 0.01 BNB covers

Free is the whole participation layer. The three tasks are ordinary social actions, registering is a wallet signature that costs no gas, and no OMC is sold at any point — there is no presale, no private round, and nothing to buy to become eligible.

The 0.01 BNB per entry is charged once, when you claim, inside the claim transaction. It is a processing charge for that transaction in the way the network charges gas, and it is deliberately not indexed to the token price: the same 0.01 BNB applies whether OMC trades at $0.01 or at $1. It is not refunded, because it funds work that has already happened — 60% to ecosystem development (audits, testnet incentives, RPC and indexer costs, the node client) and 40% to team operations. Every one of the 20 OMC in an entry still arrives in full.

This is stated in the announcement rather than in a footnote because the alternative — a number discovered at the wallet prompt — is how airdrops earn a reputation they do not recover from.

The token behind it

OMC settles compute jobs on BNB Chain. The protocol fee is 8% of settled job volume, charged to the requester: 50% burned, 30% to staked nodes, 20% to the treasury. Supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 with no inflation parameter, and emission is gated by trailing 30-day fee revenue so issuance tracks real usage rather than a calendar. Circulating supply at TGE is 65,500,000 OMC. The airdrop is the only allocation delivered in full at claim; the lock-ups sit on the investor allocations. Full detail: Tokenomics and Whitepaper.

What is not live yet

Stated plainly, because a launch post that omits this is not worth reading: there is no mainnet deployment, no token contract, no audit, and no exchange listing. Those arrive on 1 January 2027 or later. Today there is a public testnet, a working node client, and a registration flow. Treat everything else as scheduled, not shipped.

Scam warning

No OMC token exists before TGE. Any contract, presale, or "claim portal" naming OMC today is fraudulent, including any that asks you to send BNB to a wallet for gas. The team will never ask for a seed phrase or a private key. The only addresses that matter are published on omc.network and in the repository.
