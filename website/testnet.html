FAQ

Testnet Questions

Do testnet rewards convert to mainnet OMC?

Testnet tOMC has no value. However, compute contributions and reputation scores accumulated on the testnet are tracked for the Compute Mining & Node Rewards pool (50% of supply) at mainnet launch.

What hardware do I need?

Any CUDA or ROCm GPU works on the testnet. Score weighting favors TFLOPS and VRAM — RTX 3090/4090 class cards land in the top hardware tiers, and NVLink-connected rigs get priority for multi-GPU jobs.

Is my data private?

Jobs run in hardened sandboxes (gVisor by default) with no host access. Results are checked under a per-job verification policy — spot-check re-execution, 2–3 node redundancy, or hardware TEE.
