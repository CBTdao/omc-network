About this build

Barbarian Age is a small real-time strategy game from the OMC community.

Four modes

  • Classic — a single duel against the AI on a symmetric map.
  • Campaign — conquer five tribes in a row.
  • Endless — wave after wave of ever-stronger enemies.
  • Epic — an interactive-movie chapter with multiple endings.
