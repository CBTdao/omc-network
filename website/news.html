Engineering Oct 11, 2026 📌 Pinned

Contracts redeployed: the slashing and fee burns now reduce supply

Our own review found that the two “burn” paths only moved tokens to a dead address, so totalSupply() never fell — a weak mechanism doing a different job from the one we had documented. Slashing and the settlement fee now call burn() on the token, and the whole set was redeployed in a cascade (the market stores the staking address, so both moved together). Verified on-chain: one 6.0 tOMC slash cut total supply by exactly 1.2, its 20% burn share. New addresses are in the site footer and on the Testnet page.
